## Collection and reconciliation
The careers pages expose Phenom public search configuration and jobDetail data. Initial relevance-sorted HTML pagination returned 238 unique IDs against a 250 total. A single public widget search response with size 1000 reconciled all 250. The language parameter requires the actual locale (en_global / fr_CA); bare fr returned English and was rejected as a French validation. The corrected French response was verified by locale and French titles. French-only Montréal IDs are 35771 and 35672. All French Montréal descriptions were scanned for salary disclosure. Per-role English requirements were paraphrased, with French-only originals translated; bilingual text was not certified identical in every sentence.

Raw sources and collection scripts are retained privately in ignored tmp/brp-research. Published original summaries: lib/target-companies/brp-jobs.json. No copied full job descriptions are served.
