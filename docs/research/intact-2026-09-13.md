# Intact research baseline — 2026-09-13
Scope: all 21 pages of https://careers.intactfc.com/jobs, Canada / Hong Kong board. Not a worldwide group vacancy census. Requisition count 203; Canada 197; Hong Kong 6. Every canonical JD fetched; all 203 have original summaries. 26 selected roles have curated qualification gates beyond extracted signals. 195 salary ranges and 160 bonus targets captured; 30 internships / co-ops; 120 explicit hybrid tags. Locations overlap across postings.

Dataset: lib/target-companies/intact-jobs.json. Raw public-source material and collection/normalisation scripts are retained in ignored tmp/intact-research for audit, not served on the website. No résumé was accessed or used for the research. No outreach sent. No scheduled monitoring configured.

## Method
Read public page preload state (jobSearch) for listings, enumerate pages, deduplicate requisitionID, then follow originalURL. Read JobPosting JSON-LD and full description text. Retain canonical URL and UTC timestamp for every record. Write original responsibility summaries instead of copying full descriptions. Extract salary/bonus/locations and mark tools/education/experience as signals rather than automatically mandatory requirements. Curated gate notes preserve required-versus-preferred distinctions for selected roles.
